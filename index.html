Ghadiya / ఘడియ · 24-minute results

Each 24-minute card contains separate time rows at every hora change and avoid-period boundary. Red means an unfavorable result, unfavorable hora, or an avoid period; amber means mixed or uncertain; green requires a favorable result and hora with no avoid periods. Each row names its active causes. Boundary times include seconds where needed, rounded to the nearest second.

Tradition and accuracy. Rahu Kalam, Yamagandam and Gulika are calculated by dividing daylight into eight parts. Durmuhurtham uses a weekday-based one-of-15 daylight segment. Panchangam conventions can differ by region; use this chart as a planning aid, not a substitute for your family's or priest's tradition.
